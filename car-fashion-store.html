Shop details

1. Connect your data file first

Go to Settings & Data File. The first time you use the app, click "Create new data file" and save it somewhere you'll remember — like your Desktop or a "CAR FASHION" folder. Every product, bill, and setting is written straight into this .json file as you work, so there's no database to manage.

Every time you reopen this page (new browser tab, restart your PC, etc.), click "Open existing data file" and pick that same file again — this loads all your saved data back in. The app can't reconnect automatically; that's a one-click step each session.

2. Add your products

Open Inventory → + Add product. Fill in the SKU, category, name, compatible models (e.g. "Swift, i20" or "Universal"), purchase price, selling price, current stock, and a reorder level. When stock falls to or below the reorder level, that item shows up as a low-stock alert on the Dashboard and in red on the Inventory table.

Use the search bar and category chips at the top of Inventory to find items quickly. Click Edit on any row to update price or stock, or Delete to remove a product entirely.

3. Billing a customer

Open New Bill. Search or filter for a product and click it to add it to the cart on the right — click again (or use the + button) to increase quantity. Enter the customer's name and phone (optional), pick a payment mode, and type in Billed by so it's on record who handled the sale (this stays filled in for your next bill too).

Adjust any discount, and use the "Apply GST to this bill" checkbox if a sale needs to go through without tax — untick it and CGST/SGST drop out of the total and off the printed receipt.

Click "Generate bill & save" to record the sale, deduct stock, and open the print dialog for the customer's receipt. You can print to paper or "Save as PDF" from the print dialog.

4. Checking past sales

Sales History lists every bill with its date, time, customer, and total. Click Reprint on any row to bring back that invoice for printing again. The Dashboard also shows a quick view of today's sales and the most recent bills.

5. Correcting a bill

Made a mistake on a bill — wrong quantity, missed item, wrong customer details? In Sales History, click Edit on that row. You can change item quantities, remove a line, add another product, and update the discount, tax, payment mode, or who billed it.

Stock adjusts automatically as you correct it — lowering a quantity puts stock back, raising it takes more out (it'll warn you if there isn't enough left). Saving reprints the invoice marked "REVISED BILL", and the bill shows an Edited tag in Sales History.

6. Processing a return

When a customer brings an item back, click Return on that bill in Sales History. Use the +/− steppers to choose how many of each item are being returned (capped at what's actually left to return), add a reason if you like, and confirm.

This puts the returned quantity back into stock, works out the refund using that bill's own discount and tax, and prints a Credit Note referencing the original invoice. Every return is logged in the Returns tab, and the original bill picks up a Partial return or Fully returned tag so you can see its status at a glance. Revenue totals on the Dashboard and Sales History are automatically net of refunds.

7. Saving your work

Changes save to your data file automatically a moment after you make them (watch the dot next to "Save data file" at the top — green means saved). You can also click "Save data file" any time to save immediately.

If your browser doesn't support direct file saving (this affects Firefox and Safari), Settings will instead show Download and Load buttons — download after changes to keep a current backup, and load that file back in next time you open the app.

8. Backing up

Since everything lives in one .json file, backing up is as simple as copying that file to a pen drive, email, or cloud folder (Google Drive, WhatsApp to yourself, etc.) every so often. To restore, just open that copy from Settings.
